HOW TO PLAY

THE BASICS

Survive the swarm as long as you can. Your squaddy shoots on his own at whoever's nearest. You just move.

CONTROLS

Phone: drag anywhere on the screen to move.
Solo keyboard: WASD or the arrow keys.
2P Co-op: Player 1 on WASD, Player 2 on the ↑←↓→ arrow keys.
123 pick upgrades · P or Esc pauses.

LEVELLING UP

Grab the green ration tins enemies drop to fill the XP bar. Each level, pick one of three upgrades: new weapons (8 in total: SA80, Gimpy, L115 sniper, frag grenades, mortars, claymores, bayonet and orbiting scalding brews), or kit like Gucci boots and body armour.

CO-OP

If your oppo goes down, stand next to them to drag them back up. If you both go down, it's ENDEX.

PICKUPS

☕ Brew heals · £ Quid for the NAAFI shop · 📻 Arty Call clears the screen · 📦 NAAFI Wagon hoovers up every ration tin on the map.

BANTER BLAST 📢

Every kill fills your banter meter. When the 📢 button glows, tap it (or press Space) to roast everyone nearby. Enemies get stunned, take emotional damage, and go and have a little cry.

WEAPON EVOLUTIONS ⭐

Max out a weapon (level 5) and own its partner kit, then open a gold supply chest (dropped by bosses and glowing Elite enemies) to evolve it into something ridiculous:
SA80 + Beasting → Gleaming SA80 · Gimpy + Gucci Kit → Gimpy of Doom · Brews + Team Medic → The Tea Urn · Grenades + Gucci Boots → Cluster Bombs · Mortar + Scoff Radar → Full Fire Mission · Bayonet + Body Armour → Whirlwind of Pointy · L115 + Gucci Kit → One Shot, One Brew · Claymores + Gucci Boots → Minefield

NASTIES

Redcaps (Military Police) cuff you on contact so you can't move for a second. Army Chefs lob mystery stew that leaves sticky puddles: wade through and you're slowed. Grab a tin of 🫘 Compo Beans and you'll gas everything around you for 7 seconds.
Sappers run at you and blow themselves up. Shoot them early and they take their mates with them. Tanks lob shells at you: get out of the orange circle!

BATTLEFIELD EVENTS

Airdrops, Brew Breaks (the enemy freeze for tea), Pay Day, surprise Inspections (double points) and Night Exercises happen at random.

CLASSES, THEATRES & DIFFICULTY

Buy new classes in the Barracks. Unlock new theatres (Desert, Arctic, FIBUA village) by surviving longer. Selection difficulty is brutal but pays 60% more. Check your Daily Orders on the main screen for bonus quid every day.

ZOMBIE MODE

Same war, but the enemy are undead squaddies who want brains. And a brew. Mostly brains. More of them, slower, harder to put down.
