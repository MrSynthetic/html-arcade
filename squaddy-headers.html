HOW TO PLAY

THE IDEA

It's football, but you're a massive-headed squaddy. Head or boot the ball into the other lot's goal. First to 5 goals or most goals when the clock runs out. Level tie = golden goal (next goal wins).

CONTROLS

Phone: on-screen buttons — move, jump, boot, and the special when your 📢 meter is full.
1 player keyboard: AD or ←→ move, W/↑ jump, S/Space boot, E/Shift special.
2 player keyboard: P1 AD move W jump S boot Shift special · P2 ←→ move ↑ jump ↓ boot / special.

SPECIALS ("ISSUES")

Touch the ball to fill your meter. Each squaddy has a signature move: artillery strike, smoke grenade, a beasting (giant head) or a cheeky NAAFI break that freezes the other lot.

POWER-UPS

Crates parachute onto the pitch. Hit one with the ball for a random effect — big ball, tiny opponent, low gravity, super boot and more.
